Anomaly Hunt

A browser game for practising two core BI skills: spotting anomalies in a time series and calling a forecast.

  • Anomaly Hunt mode is a timed challenge that scores your picks against statistical detectors.
  • Forecast Call mode asks you to pick the next value and stake confidence on your answer.

Where Winds Meet GvG Manager · Discord bot

WWMG

A Guild vs Guild management system that runs inside Discord: player registration, events, rosters and commander admin.

  • Generates rosters automatically from past team assignments, teammate relationships and sub-squad preferences.
  • Stores events and registrations in Supabase/PostgreSQL, with versioned SQL migrations.
  • Role-based access separates member commands from commander commands.
